systemd: 
  systemd is a system and service manager for Linux operating systems. It's the first process that starts when your VPS boots up and manages everything else that runs on your server.
  Think of it as the "master controller" or "orchestrator" of your VPS. It's responsible for starting, stopping, monitoring, and logging all other processes (services) on your Linux system.
  When you deploy applications on a VPS, you need them to run continuously, survive reboots, and be manageable. systemd is the tool that makes this possible.

systemctl is the command-line tool used to control and interact with systemd,
Think of systemd as the "mother of all processes" (PID 1) that manages everything else, and systemctl as your remote control to command it.
